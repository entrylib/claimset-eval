DEFINITIONS
D1 [method] smooth ES: The ES class of the Python package smooth (Ivan Svetunkov) with the model form fixed; estimated smoothing parameters by likelihood; usual bounds (alpha, beta, gamma in [0,1], beta at most alpha, gamma at most 1 minus alpha); initial states optimised (initial='optimal') or by backcasting (initial='backcasting', its default). Two arms: optimised and backcasting.
D2 [method] statsforecast ETS: The ets_f function of statsforecast (Nixtla) with the model form fixed; likelihood loss (opt_crit 'lik'); smoothing parameters bounded to [0.0001, 0.9999], with beta's upper bound set to the starting alpha (about 0.2 for non-seasonal models); initial states optimised.
D3 [data] ETS innovations-form data: Simulated series of length T in {50, 100, 250, 500} from ETS(A,N,N), ETS(A,A,N) and ETS(A,N,A) with m=12 in innovations form, additive Gaussian errors with sigma=1, l_0=100, b_0=1 and s_j=10 sin(2 pi j/12) for j=0,...,11; no burn-in. ETS(A,N,N): $y_t = l_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t$, $l_t = l_{t-1} + \alpha \varepsilon_t$. ETS(A,A,N): $y_t = l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t$, $l_t = l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \alpha \varepsilon_t$, $b_t = b_{t-1} + \beta \varepsilon_t$. ETS(A,N,A): $y_t = l_{t-1} + s_{t-m} + \varepsilon_t$, $l_t = l_{t-1} + \alpha \varepsilon_t$, $s_t = s_{t-m} + \gamma \varepsilon_t$; $\varepsilon_t \sim N(0,1)$ i.i.d.
D4 [variable] bias of smoothing parameter estimates: Mean over successful replicates of (estimate minus true value) of a smoothing parameter.
D5 [variable] RMSE of smoothing parameter estimates: Root mean squared error of a smoothing-parameter estimate around its true value, over successful replicates.
D6 [variable] MAE of smoothing parameter estimates: Mean absolute error of a smoothing-parameter estimate around its true value, over successful replicates.
D7 [design] Monte Carlo replicate design: 5 processes x 4 series lengths x 300 replicates per cell. Each replicate has its own fixed seed derived from the base seed 2026; the three arms are fitted to the same series. Fit failures and non-finite estimates are counted, not dropped.
D8 [variable] share of estimates on a bound: Share of successful replicates whose estimate lies within 0.001 of a bound of the implementation's parameter range: smooth 0 or 1 (beta also at alpha); statsforecast 0.0001 or 0.9999 (beta also at its cap).
