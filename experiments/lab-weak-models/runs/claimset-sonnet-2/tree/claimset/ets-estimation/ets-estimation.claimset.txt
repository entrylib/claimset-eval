DEFINITIONS
D1 [method] smooth ES, optimised initials: smooth 1.2.0 ES(model=<form>, initial='optimal', loss='likelihood', bounds='usual'). Each fits the data-generating form (no model selection), with the same innovations-form equations as the generating process, for the form fixed to the generating one:
$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \\ b_t &= b_{t-1} + \beta\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + s_{t-m} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \\ s_t &= s_{t-m} + \gamma\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$
with the smoothing parameters $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ estimated by maximum likelihood of the Gaussian innovations model. The initial states are estimated jointly with the smoothing parameters. Parameter bounds: bounds='usual'.
D2 [method] smooth ES, backcasting: smooth 1.2.0 ES(model=<form>, initial='backcasting', loss='likelihood', bounds='usual'), smooth's default. Each fits the data-generating form (no model selection), with the same innovations-form equations as the generating process, for the form fixed to the generating one:
$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \\ b_t &= b_{t-1} + \beta\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + s_{t-m} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \\ s_t &= s_{t-m} + \gamma\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$
with the smoothing parameters $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ estimated by maximum likelihood of the Gaussian innovations model. The initial states are obtained by backcasting; only the smoothing parameters are optimised. Parameter bounds: bounds='usual'.
D3 [method] statsforecast AutoETS: statsforecast 2.1.1 AutoETS(season_length=m, model=<form>, damped=False) with the form fixed to the generating one (no selection). Each fits the data-generating form (no model selection), with the same innovations-form equations as the generating process, for the form fixed to the generating one:
$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \\ b_t &= b_{t-1} + \beta\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + s_{t-m} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \\ s_t &= s_{t-m} + \gamma\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$
with the smoothing parameters $\alpha$, $\beta$, $\gamma$ estimated by maximum likelihood of the Gaussian innovations model. The initial states are optimised jointly with the smoothing parameters. Bounds: lower 1e-4 and upper 0.9999 for $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\gamma$, with $\beta\le\alpha$, $\gamma\le 1-\alpha$ and admissibility.
D4 [data] ETS data generating process: Simulated series from ETS in its innovations form with additive Gaussian errors $\varepsilon_t \sim N(0,1)$ independent, started from $l_0=100$, $b_0=1$ and seasonal indices $s_j=10\sin(2\pi j/12)$ for $j=0,\dots,11$, with no burn-in. Three forms:

ETS(A,N,N):
$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$
ETS(A,A,N):
$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + b_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \\ b_t &= b_{t-1} + \beta\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$
ETS(A,N,A) with period $m=12$:
$$\begin{aligned} y_t &= l_{t-1} + s_{t-m} + \varepsilon_t \\ l_t &= l_{t-1} + \alpha\,\varepsilon_t \\ s_t &= s_{t-m} + \gamma\,\varepsilon_t \end{aligned}$$
Parameter settings: ETS(A,N,N) with $\alpha=0.1$ and with $\alpha=0.5$; ETS(A,A,N) with $(\alpha,\beta)=(0.3,0.05)$ and with $(0.5,0.2)$; ETS(A,N,A) with $(\alpha,\gamma)=(0.2,0.1)$. Together the five settings have 8 parameter cells (setting and smoothing parameter). Series lengths $T\in\{50,100,250,500\}$, 300 replicates per setting and length. Each setting and length has its own random stream, seeded from the run seed, the setting index and $T$.
D5 [variable] bias: Mean over successful replicates of the estimate minus the true value of a smoothing parameter, for one method, setting and series length.
D6 [variable] RMSE: Square root of the mean over successful replicates of the squared (estimate minus true value) of a smoothing parameter.
D7 [variable] MAE: Mean over successful replicates of the absolute value of (estimate minus true value) of a smoothing parameter.
D8 [concept] consistency: Consistency of an estimator in the sense used here: the RMSE of a smoothing parameter estimate is lower at series length $T=500$ than at $T=50$ in a given setting. This checks the direction of change between two lengths, not the rate of convergence or the limit.
D9 [variable] RMSE ratio: For a setting, smoothing parameter and series length: the RMSE of smooth ES with optimised initial states divided by the RMSE of statsforecast AutoETS. There are 32 such cells (8 parameter cells, 4 lengths).
D10 [variable] estimate on a bound: A replicate whose estimate of a smoothing parameter lies within $10^{-3}$ of a lower or upper bound of its admissible region (for $\beta$: $0$ or $\alpha$; for $\gamma$: $0$ or $1-\alpha$; for $\alpha$: $0$ or $1$), counted as in the run's script.

LIMITATIONS
L1 [model_specification; affects C1, C3, C5; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation none] In the ETS(A,N,A) setting the estimates of gamma often lie on a bound: at series length 500, 286 of 300 replicates for smooth ES with backcasting, 118 for smooth ES with optimised initials and 25 for statsforecast AutoETS; the bias of the smooth estimates stays near -0.09 and -0.06, and the cause (bounds, parametrisation or the optimiser) was not investigated.
L2 [computational; affects C1, C2, C3; reduces_transferability; mitigation none] Each package was run in one version (smooth 1.2.0, statsforecast 2.1.1) with the model form fixed to the generating one and default optimiser settings, so the results do not transfer to other versions or to automatic model selection.
L3 [researcher_position; affects C1, C2, C3; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation partial] The guarantor is the author and maintainer of the smooth package under evaluation; the simulation design and the analysis were fixed in a plan before the run, and every result is recorded, including those against smooth.
