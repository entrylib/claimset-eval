DEFINITIONS
D1 [method] smooth ES, optimised initials: smooth 1.2.0 ES(model=<form>, initial='optimal', loss='likelihood', bounds='usual'); smoothing parameters estimated by maximum likelihood with the initial states estimated jointly.
D2 [method] smooth ES, backcasting: smooth 1.2.0 ES(model=<form>, initial='backcasting', loss='likelihood', bounds='usual'): smooth's default; initial states obtained by backcasting, only smoothing parameters optimised.
D3 [method] statsforecast AutoETS: statsforecast 2.1.1 AutoETS(season_length=m, model=<form>, damped=False) with the form fixed to the generating one (no selection); maximum likelihood with optimised initial states; bounds lower 1e-4, upper 0.9999 for alpha, beta, gamma, beta<=alpha, gamma<=1-alpha, and admissibility.
D4 [data] ETS data generating process: Innovations ETS with additive Gaussian errors, sigma=1, started from l0=100, b0=1, s_j=10 sin(2 pi j/12), no burn-in; ETS(A,N,N) alpha 0.1 and 0.5; ETS(A,A,N) (alpha,beta) (0.3,0.05) and (0.5,0.2); ETS(A,N,A) m=12 (alpha,gamma)=(0.2,0.1); lengths 50,100,250,500; R replicates per cell.
D5 [variable] bias: Mean over successful replicates of (estimate minus true value) of a smoothing parameter.
D6 [variable] RMSE: Square root of the mean over successful replicates of the squared (estimate minus true value) of a smoothing parameter.
D7 [variable] MAE: Mean over successful replicates of the absolute value of (estimate minus true value) of a smoothing parameter.
D8 [concept] consistency: The RMSE of an estimator falls towards zero as series length T grows; here assessed on T in {50,100,250,500} by the RMSE at T=500 against the RMSE at T=50 and the trend across the four lengths.

C4 [descriptive; supporting; assumption (statistical, unchecked)]
  The two implementations optimise comparable objectives: both maximise the Gaussian likelihood of the innovations form (smooth loss='likelihood', statsforecast opt_crit='lik'), so differences in estimates come from optimiser, bounds and initial-state handling.
C5 [descriptive; supporting; assumption (statistical, unchecked)]
  alpha, beta, gamma have the same meaning in both packages (innovations/error-correction form, b_t=b_{t-1}+beta*e_t, s_t=s_{t-m}+gamma*e_t), so estimates are compared to the true values without conversion; to be checked by the data (bias of both near zero at T=500) not assumed unchecked.
