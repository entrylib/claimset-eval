CLAIMSET Sequential C–O decarboxylative vinylation/C–H arylation of cyclic oxalates via a nickel-catalyzed multicomponent radical cascade
licence CC-BY-3.0; converted from 10.1039/d0sc01471k published by Huan Li, Lei Guo, Xiaoliang Feng, Liping Huo, Shengqing Zhu, Lingling Chu; contributions C1
declarations funding: none | competing interests: Not stated in the converted text.

DEFINITIONS
D1 [method] vinylation/arylation cascade: One-step reaction of a cyclic alkyl oxalate (caesium salt), a terminal alkyne and an aryl halide: decarboxylative alkyl radical addition to the alkyne, 1,5-hydrogen atom transfer from the ring to the vinyl radical, and nickel-catalysed arylation of the ring carbon radical.
D2 [method] standard conditions: Alkyne 0.1 mmol, cyclic oxalate 3.0 equiv, aryl halide 2.0 equiv, Ir[dF(CF3)ppy]2(dtbbpy)PF6 2 mol%, NiCl2(Py)4 20 mol%, dtbbpy 20 mol%, bis(4-methoxyphenyl)methanone 10 mol%, DMSO 0.05 M, 37 °C, 90 W blue LEDs.
D3 [substance] 1,3-vinylarylated cycloalkanes: Products carrying a trans-alkenyl group and an aryl group on carbons 1 and 3 of a saturated ring, formed as roughly 1:1 to 1:1.2 mixtures of diastereomers.
D4 [variable] yield: Isolated yield of product based on the alkyne, unless the source marks it as a crude 1H NMR yield.
D5 [intervention] control reactions: The standard conditions with one component removed: photocatalyst, nickel catalyst, light, ligand or additive. [members D6, D7, D8]
D6 [intervention] no photocatalyst: Standard conditions without the iridium photocatalyst.
D7 [intervention] no nickel: Standard conditions without the nickel catalyst.
D8 [intervention] no light: Standard conditions in the dark.
D9 [concept] vinyl radical intermediate: The radical formed when the alkyl radical adds to the alkyne, proposed to abstract a hydrogen atom from carbon 5 of its own chain (1,5-HAT).
D10 [method] radical clock experiment: Reaction under standard conditions with ethynylcyclopropane in place of the alkyne; ring opening to an allene indicates a vinyl radical.
D11 [substance] substrate classes: The groups of the scope tables: cyclic oxalates, alkynes, aryl bromides, heteroaryl halides, aryl iodides and chlorides. [members D12, D13, D14, D15]
D12 [substance] cyclic oxalates scope: Products 4-14: varied five- and six-membered carbo- and heterocyclic oxalates.
D13 [substance] alkyne scope: Products 19-29: varied terminal aliphatic alkynes, including late-stage examples.
D14 [substance] aryl bromide scope: Products 30-35: varied aryl bromides.
D15 [substance] heteroaryl and other aryl halide scope: Products 36-42: heteroaryl halides, aryl iodides and aryl chlorides.

CLAIMS
C1 [descriptive; principal; support: empirical_quant E1 (E1.R1); limitations L1, L2]
  Under the [[D2:standard conditions]], the [[D1:vinylation/arylation cascade]] gives [[D3:1,3-vinylarylated cycloalkanes]] from five- and six-membered cyclic oxalates, terminal aliphatic alkynes and aryl halides in 36% to 77% [[D4:yield]].
C2 [causal; secondary; not_applicable; support: empirical_quant E2]
  Compared with the [[D2:standard conditions]], each of the [[D5:control reactions]] that removes the photocatalyst, the nickel catalyst or light gives 0% [[D4:yield]].
C3 [mechanistic; secondary; not_applicable; support: empirical_quant E3; limitations L3]
  The [[D1:vinylation/arylation cascade]] proceeds through a [[D9:vinyl radical intermediate]], as the [[D10:radical clock experiment]] gives the ring-opened allene.

LIMITATIONS
L1 [sampling; affects C1; reduces_transferability; mitigation none] Four- and seven-membered, bicyclic and linear oxalates and internal alkynes fail, so the scope is limited to five- and six-membered rings and terminal alkynes.
L2 [measurement; affects C1; reduces_transferability; mitigation none] Products form as near 1:1 diastereomer mixtures; yields combine both diastereomers.
L3 [other; affects C3; could_reverse; mitigation none] The authors note that a nickel(0) oxidative-addition pathway cannot be excluded.
