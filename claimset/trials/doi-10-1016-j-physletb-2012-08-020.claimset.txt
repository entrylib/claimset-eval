DEFINITIONS
D1 [other] new neutral boson: The particle observed in this search: neutral, decaying to pairs of photons, Z bosons and W bosons, with a mass near 126 GeV.
D2 [theory] Standard Model Higgs boson: The scalar boson predicted by the Standard Model's mechanism of electroweak symmetry breaking.
D3 [data] 2011 data: Proton-proton collisions at a centre-of-mass energy of 7 TeV recorded by ATLAS in 2011, about 4.8 fb^-1.
D4 [data] 2012 data: Proton-proton collisions at a centre-of-mass energy of 8 TeV recorded by ATLAS in 2012, about 5.8 fb^-1.
D5 [other] decay channels: The final states searched: H->ZZ(*)->4l, H->gamma gamma and H->WW(*)->l nu l nu. [members D6, D7, D8]
D6 [other] H->ZZ(*)->4l: Decay to two Z bosons, one possibly off-shell, each decaying to a pair of charged leptons.
D7 [other] H->gamma gamma: Decay to two photons.
D8 [other] H->WW(*)->l nu l nu: Decay to two W bosons, each decaying to a charged lepton and a neutrino.
D9 [variable] mass: Rest mass of the new particle, from the combined fit of the 4l and gamma gamma channels.
D10 [variable] local significance: Significance of the excess over the background-only hypothesis at the fitted mass, in standard deviations, without the look-elsewhere correction.
D11 [variable] signal strength: Ratio of the observed signal rate to the rate the Standard Model predicts for its Higgs boson at the fitted mass (mu).
D12 [design] profile likelihood search: Search for an excess over the expected background in each channel, combined in a profile-likelihood test of the background-only hypothesis, with mass scanned over 110-600 GeV.

CLAIMS
C1 [descriptive; principal; support: empirical_quant E1 (E1.R2)]
  ATLAS observes a [[D1:new neutral boson]] with a [[D10:local significance]] of 5.9 standard deviations over the background-only expectation, in the combined [[D3:2011 data]] and [[D4:2012 data]].
C2 [descriptive; principal; support: empirical_quant E1 (E1.R1)]
  The [[D9:mass]] of the [[D1:new neutral boson]] is 126.0 GeV, with a statistical uncertainty of 0.4 GeV and a systematic uncertainty of 0.4 GeV.
C3 [descriptive; principal; support: empirical_quant E1 (E1.R4)]
  The [[D11:signal strength]] of the [[D1:new neutral boson]] is 1.4 plus or minus 0.3, consistent with the [[D2:Standard Model Higgs boson]].
C4 [descriptive; secondary; support: empirical_quant E1]
  A [[D2:Standard Model Higgs boson]] is excluded at 95% confidence level over most of the searched [[D9:mass]] range, except a window around the new particle's mass.

LIMITATIONS
L1 [other; affects C1; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation addressed] The local significance does not account for the look-elsewhere effect over 110-600 GeV; the global significance over that range is 5.1 standard deviations.
