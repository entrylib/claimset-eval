DEFINITIONS
D1 [intervention] dietary support: Seven individual sessions of about 60 minutes with a clinical dietitian over 12 weeks (weekly for four, then fortnightly), promoting a modified Mediterranean diet (ModiMedDiet), added to usual treatment.
D2 [intervention] social support: Befriending: seven sessions of the same schedule and length with a trained person, without dietary content; the attention control.
D3 [population] adults with moderate to severe depression and poor diet: Adults 18 or older with a current major depressive episode (DSM-IV-TR), MADRS 18 or higher, and a Dietary Screening Tool score of 75 or less; 67 randomised at two sites in Victoria, Australia.
D4 [variable] MADRS: Montgomery-Asberg Depression Rating Scale, clinician-rated, 0-60; higher is more severe. The primary outcome, at 12 weeks.
D5 [variable] remission: MADRS below 10 at 12 weeks.
D6 [variable] HADS depression: Depression subscale of the Hospital Anxiety and Depression Scale, self-report.
D7 [design] single-blind parallel-group trial: Two-arm, 12-week randomised trial; computer-generated sequence in blocks of four; outcome assessors and statistician blinded, participants not; analysis by intention to treat with a mixed model for repeated measures (missing at random).
D8 [intervention] trial arm: The arms of the trial. [members D1, D2]

CLAIMS
C1 [causal; principal; support: empirical_quant E1 (E1.R1, E1.R2); limitations L1, L2, L3, L5; planning confirmatory_with_deviations; outcome supported]
  In [[D3:adults with moderate to severe depression and poor diet]], [[D1:dietary support]] reduced [[D4:MADRS]] scores at 12 weeks more than [[D2:social support]], by 7.1 points.
  test: frequentist; alpha 0.05; test mixed model for repeated measures: difference between arms in MADRS change from baseline to 12 weeks; sides two-sided; correction none (single primary outcome); sample 176 planned; 67 randomised; supported if MADRS falls more with dietary support than with social support at 12 weeks, p < alpha; not supported if MADRS falls less with dietary support, or the difference is not significant with adequate power; verdict judged
  plan: registered B1; planned 2012-02-29T00:00:00Z; anchor registry
C2 [causal; principal; support: empirical_quant E1; limitations L2]
  More participants reached [[D5:remission]] with [[D1:dietary support]] than with [[D2:social support]] (32.3% against 8.0%) among [[D3:adults with moderate to severe depression and poor diet]].
C3 [causal; secondary; support: empirical_quant E1 (E1.R4); limitations L4]
  [[D1:dietary support]] reduced [[D6:HADS depression]] scores more than [[D2:social support]] in [[D3:adults with moderate to severe depression and poor diet]].

LIMITATIONS
L1 [measurement; affects C1, C2, C3; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation partial] Participants could not be blinded and knew the intervention's nature from advertising, so expectation may contribute to the effect.
L2 [sample_size; affects C1, C2; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation none] 67 were randomised against a planned 176, which may inflate the effect size.
L3 [data_quality; affects C1, C2; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation partial] Completion differed between arms (94% with dietary support, 74% with social support).
L4 [analytic_flexibility; affects C3; could_reverse; mitigation none] Secondary outcomes were not adjusted for multiple comparisons.
L5 [deviation; affects C1; reduces_precision; mitigation none] The trial randomised 67 participants of the 176 its registration planned.
