CLAIMSET The effect of preregistration on trust in empirical research findings: results of a registered report

DEFINITIONS
D1 [intervention] preregistration status: What the fictional study's description said about its planning: no preregistration, preregistered (PR), or a registered report (RR). [members D2, D3, D4]
D2 [intervention] no preregistration: The described study was not preregistered.
D3 [intervention] preregistration: The described study was preregistered (PR).
D4 [intervention] registered report: The described study was a registered report (RR), its protocol peer reviewed before data collection.
D5 [intervention] author familiarity: Whether the described study's author was presented as familiar to the participant or not.
D6 [variable] trust in the findings: Single item, 'How much do you trust the results of this study?', on a 1-9 slider. [measures D9]
D7 [population] psychology researchers: Authors of psychology articles indexed in Web of Science, 2013-2017 (with 2009-2010 added); 9,019 contacted, 654 completed, 209 analysed after manipulation checks.
D8 [design] Bayesian 3 x 2 between-subjects ANOVA: Each participant read one of six fictional scenarios (preregistration status x familiarity) and rated trust; Bayesian ANOVA in JASP with default priors; inclusion Bayes factors per effect.
D9 [concept] trust in research: The confidence a researcher places in a reported finding; here measured by D6.

CLAIMS
C1 [causal; principal; support: empirical_quant E1 (E1.R1, E1.R2); limitations L1, L2, L3, L4; planning confirmatory_with_deviations; outcome inconclusive]
  Among [[D7:psychology researchers]], [[D1:preregistration status]] ([[D3:preregistration]] or a [[D4:registered report]]) did not clearly change [[D6:trust in the findings]] relative to [[D2:no preregistration]]: the evidence was ambiguous, leaning towards no effect.
  test: bayesian; prior default priors of the BayesFactor package (Rouder and Morey); threshold Jeffreys' scale of evidence; sample 80 per cell, 480 in all; supported if the inclusion Bayes factor for preregistration status gives evidence for an effect on Jeffreys' scale; not supported if the inclusion Bayes factor for preregistration status gives evidence against an effect on Jeffreys' scale; verdict judged
  plan: registered B1; anchor registry
C2 [causal; principal; support: empirical_quant E1 (E1.R3, E1.R4); limitations L1, L2, L4; planning confirmatory_with_deviations; outcome inconclusive]
  Among [[D7:psychology researchers]], [[D5:author familiarity]] did not increase [[D6:trust in the findings]], alone or with [[D1:preregistration status]].
  test: bayesian; prior default priors of the BayesFactor package; threshold Jeffreys' scale of evidence; sample 80 per cell; supported if the inclusion Bayes factor for familiarity gives evidence for an effect on Jeffreys' scale; not supported if the inclusion Bayes factor for familiarity gives evidence against an effect on Jeffreys' scale; verdict judged
  plan: registered B1; anchor registry
C3 [causal; secondary; support: empirical_quant E2 (E2.R1, E2.R2); planning exploratory]
  Without the manipulation-check exclusions, the evidence on [[D1:preregistration status]] and [[D5:author familiarity]] among [[D7:psychology researchers]] is qualitatively the same: no effect on [[D6:trust in the findings]] is supported.

EVIDENCE
E1 [quant; design experiment; data_origin primary; n 209; preregistered] Registered report: online between-subjects experiment with psychology researchers rating trust in one of six fictional study descriptions (N = 209 after manipulation checks).
  setup: studied D1, D5; cases D7; procedure D8; lens D6

  derived from E1 (reanalysis)

LIMITATIONS
L1 [sample_size; affects C1, C2; reduces_precision; mitigation none] 209 participants were analysed against a target of 480, with cells of 12 to 84.
L2 [data_quality; affects C1, C2; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation partial] 68% of completed responses failed the manipulation checks and were excluded, possibly because the manipulations were too subtle.
L3 [measurement; affects C1; could_bias_magnitude; mitigation none] Trust was measured by a single item of unknown reliability and validity.
L4 [deviation; affects C1, C2; reduces_precision; mitigation none] The sampling departed from the registered plan: after the first wave left too few usable responses, a second wave of researchers was drawn from Web of Science and invited. The authors report this as the one deviation.
