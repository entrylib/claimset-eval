---
title: "Croston's method on spare-part demand at Velmar Rail"
author: "Working note, 2026"
---

We compared Croston's method with simple exponential smoothing on 312 spare parts of Velmar Rail, observed monthly from 2018 to 2024. Croston's method had a lower mean absolute error on parts with more than half the months at zero demand (1.84 against 2.07) and a higher one on the other parts (3.12 against 2.95).

| Group | Croston MAE | SES MAE |
|---|---|---|
| Parts with mostly zero demand | 1.84 | 2.07 |
| Other parts | 3.12 | 2.95 |
