---
title: "Hierarchical reconciliation of call-centre volumes"
author: "Working note, 2026"
---

Hourly call volumes of a call centre with 3 queues were forecast per queue and in total, then reconciled by ordinary least squares. Reconciliation lowered the root mean squared error of the total by 6% and of the smallest queue by 2%, over 8 weeks of hourly data.

| Series | RMSE reduction |
|---|---|
| Total | 6% |
| Smallest queue | 2% |
